# OPERA Cloud Glossary

Complete terminology reference for OPERA Cloud system.

---

## A

**ADR (Average Daily Rate)** - Mean room rate for a given period. Calculated as: Total room revenue ÷ number of rooms sold.

**Amenities** - Services or items provided to guests (WiFi, parking, breakfast, fitness center, etc.).

**API (Application Programming Interface)** - Technical interface allowing external systems to connect to OPERA Cloud (e.g., channel managers, payment processors).

**Arrival** - Guest check-in date and time.

**A/R (Accounts Receivable)** - Outstanding guest folios (unpaid bills) requiring collection.

**Audit Trail** - System record of all changes made to data (who, what, when) for compliance and troubleshooting.

---

## B

**Back Office** - External accounting system (e.g., Oracle General Ledger) that receives hotel financial data from OPERA Cloud.

**Batch** - Group of records processed together (e.g., batch import of rates, batch export of fiscal data).

**Block** - Group reservation (e.g., tour group, conference attendees, wedding guests). Managed as single entity with individual guest rooms.

**Business Date** - Hotel's operational date (may differ from calendar date; used for night audit close).

**Business Events** - Real-time notifications when specific actions occur (e.g., "Guest checked out", "Folio created").

---

## C

**Cashier** - Front Desk staff member handling guest payments and cash management.

**Cashiering** - Process of collecting payments from guests and managing cash drawer.

**Channel Manager** - System connecting hotel to multiple Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) like Booking.com, Expedia for rate and availability distribution.

**Charge** - Cost added to guest folio (room rate, meal, phone, resort fee, etc.).

**Check-In** - Guest arrival process (verify reservation, collect payment, issue key, assign room).

**Check-Out** - Guest departure process (settle folio, collect payment, retrieve key, clean room).

**Comp (Complimentary)** - Free service or refund given to guest as goodwill or service recovery.

**Concierge** - Staff member assisting guests with arrangements (dining, tours, transportation, etc.).

**Corporate Rate** - Negotiated room rate for companies with employee travel programs.

---

## D

**Dashboard** - Home screen showing key metrics and quick access actions.

**Departure** - Guest check-out date.

**Distribution** - Process of sending room rates and availability to channels and OTAs.

**Double Occupancy** - Room occupied by 2 guests.

---

## E

**EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer)** - Automated payment processing (ACH, wire transfer).

**Enterprise** - Large hotel chain or multi-property operator.

**Exchange** - System module handling integrations with external systems (IFC8, channel managers, payment processors).

---

## F

**Fiscal Compliance** - Meeting regulatory requirements for financial reporting (SAF-T in Portugal, SII in Spain, etc.).

**Folio** - Guest's account/bill during and after stay. Shows all charges and payments.

**Folio Window** - Individual billing section within a folio (e.g., room window, restaurant window).

**Forecast** - Projected occupancy, revenue, and demand based on reservations and market trends.

---

## G

**Group** - Multiple guests booked under one reservation (also called "Block").

**Group Rate** - Negotiated rate for group reservations.

---

## H

**Housekeeping** - Department responsible for cleaning and maintaining guest rooms.

**House Status** - Overall occupancy status of hotel (percentage of rooms occupied).

**Hub** - Central property in multi-property organization that manages enterprise functions.

---

## I

**IFC8** - Integration Framework allowing connection between OPERA and external systems (back office, PMS, etc.).

**Incidental** - Miscellaneous charge to folio (resort fee, damage, service charge, etc.).

**Inventory** - Available rooms by type and date.

**Inventory Control** - System preventing overselling of rooms beyond physical availability.

---

## K

**Key Card** - Access card for guest room (also called "key," "room key," "access card").

---

## L

**Loyalty** - Program rewarding frequent guests with points, upgrades, or benefits.

**Loyalty Member** - Guest enrolled in hotel's loyalty program.

---

## M

**Market Segment** - Classification of guest type (Corporate, Leisure, Government, Group, etc.).

**Micros** - Legacy property management system (historical reference; replaced by OPERA).

**Mobile Check-In** - Guest check-in process completed via mobile app before arrival.

**Month-End Close** - Financial close procedure finalizing month's records for accounting.

---

## N

**Night Audit** - End-of-day process reconciling guest folios, posting charges, and preparing financial close.

**No-Show** - Guest with reservation who doesn't arrive by specified time and didn't cancel.

**Non-Refundable Rate** - Rate with no refund if guest cancels.

---

## O

**Occupancy** - Percentage of rooms occupied (e.g., 85% occupancy = 85 of 100 rooms occupied).

**OOO (Out of Order)** - Room unavailable due to maintenance or damage.

**OPE (Online Property Evaluation)** - Oracle platform for property data and administration.

**OPH (Oracle Property Hub)** - Central management interface for multi-property operations.

**OPERA** - Oracle Property Enterprise Resource Administration (the PMS system).

**OTA (Online Travel Agency)** - Third-party booking platform (Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, etc.).

**Outstanding Folio** - Guest bill not yet paid.

**Oversell** - Selling more room nights than physically available (managed via inventory controls).

---

## P

**Parking** - Valet or self-park vehicle storage (may be complimentary or charged).

**Payment Method** - How guest pays (credit card, cash, check, account, etc.).

**PCI Compliance** - Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard for secure payment processing.

**PMS (Property Management System)** - Hotel software system (OPERA is an example).

**Pre-Arrival** - Period before guest check-in (24-48 hours prior).

**Preference** - Guest's stated choices (room location, bed type, late checkout, etc.).

**Profile** - Master record of guest with contact info, preferences, history, payment methods.

---

## R

**Rate** - Room price per night.

**Rate Plan** - Pricing structure defining how room rates are sold (corporate, leisure, group, etc.).

**Reconciliation** - Process of verifying that records match (e.g., folio total vs. system total).

**Reservation** - Booking confirming guest's stay (name, dates, room type, rate, payment).

**Revenue** - Income from room sales, food, beverage, and other services.

**RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)** - Key metric: Total room revenue ÷ Total rooms available. Combines occupancy and ADR.

**Room Status** - Current condition of room (Occupied, Vacant, Dirty, OOO, Inspected, Locked).

**Room Type** - Category of room (Standard, Deluxe, Suite, Accessible, etc.).

---

## S

**SAF-T (Standard Audit File - Tax)** - Portuguese/Spanish fiscal export format for tax compliance.

**Segment** - Classification used to group reservations by type (corporate, leisure, group, etc.).

**Shift** - Work period (morning 7am-3pm, afternoon 3pm-11pm, night 11pm-7am).

**Single Occupancy** - Room occupied by 1 guest.

**SiteMinder** - Channel manager system for distributing rates to OTAs.

**Stay** - Guest's entire visit from check-in to check-out.

**Surcharge** - Additional charge (resort fee, parking, early checkout fee, etc.).

**SynXis** - Central reservation system and channel manager (Sabre).

---

## T

**Tax** - Government-mandated charge added to room rate (varies by jurisdiction).

**Third Party** - External system or company (OTA, payment processor, accounting system, etc.).

**Timeout** - Automatic logout after period of inactivity (usually 30 minutes).

**Transaction** - Single financial event (charge, payment, refund, adjustment).

---

## U

**Upgrade** - Moving guest to higher category room than reserved (usually complimentary goodwill).

---

## V

**VIP** - Very Important Person (guest given special attention, upgrades, or services).

**Void** - Cancel or reverse a transaction.

---

## W

**Waitlist** - Queue of guests waiting for unavailable room type or arrival date.

**Workflow** - Series of steps to complete a process (e.g., check-in workflow).

### Rates & Revenue
- **Rate Plan** - Pricing strategy
- **ADR** - Average daily rate
- **RevPAR** - Revenue per available room
- **Occupancy** - Percentage booked
- **Forecast** - Demand prediction

- **Check-In** - Guest arrival
- **Check-Out** - Guest departure
- **Night Audit** - Daily close
- **Housekeeping** - Room cleaning
- **Cashiering** - Payment processing

### Guests
- **Reservation** - Booking
- **Profile** - Guest record
- **Folio** - Guest bill
- **Loyalty** - Rewards program
- **Segment** - Guest classification

- **OTA** - Online travel agency

- **IFC8** - System integration
