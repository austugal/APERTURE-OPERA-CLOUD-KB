# OPERA Cloud PMS Training Schedule — 40 Hours / 5 Days

Source: Official Oracle PMS Training and Q&A schedule, 40-hour delivery over 5 consecutive days.

## Pre-training requirement

Digital Learning must be scheduled within 1 week of the Oracle trainer's arrival on property. Recommended completion before go-live day: 100%. Tracked via the Training Administration Portal (TAP). Access to TAP must be requested before training week starts.

## Daily structure

Each day: 8 hours total. Morning block 09:00 to 13:00 (4 hours). Lunch break. Afternoon block 14:00 to 18:00 (4 hours). Total: 40 hours across 5 days.

## Day-by-day breakdown

### Day 1 — Foundation and Profiles

**Morning 09:00 to 13:00 — Introduction to OPERA Cloud and Profiles**
- Trainer introduction
- Introduction to OPERA Cloud
- Navigating OPERA Cloud, clearing cache
- Profiles

**Afternoon 14:00 to 18:00 — Profiles and Look to Book**
- Profile
- Look to Book

### Day 2 — Reservations

**Morning — Individual Reservations and Inventory**
- Individual Reservations (create, edit)
- Reservations Options
- Inventory

**Afternoon — Arrivals and In-House**
- Guest Arrivals
- Guest In-House
- Front Desk Workspace
- Room Diary

### Day 3 — Blocks and Cashiering

**Morning — Blocks**
- Availability Control
- Creating a Block
- Block Header and Other Options
- Loading and Editing Rates

**Afternoon — Cashiering**
- PIN Set for Cashiering
- Posting and correcting charges

### Day 4 — Cashiering and Audit

**Morning — Cashiering**
- Cashiering Miscellaneous
- Generating an Invoice

**Afternoon — Cashiering, Night Audit, Reports**
- Cashier Audit
- End of Day Pre-Check
- End of Day

### Day 5 — Housekeeping, Reports, Wrap-up

**Morning — Housekeeping and Reports**
- Rooms Status Change
- Task Assignment
- Reports
- Reports Scheduler

**Afternoon — Q&A and Wrap-up**
- Wrap-up session
- Open Q&A

## Audience expectations per day

| Day | Primary audience |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | All operational staff (front office, reservations, sales) |
| Day 2 | Front office, reservations |
| Day 3 | Reservations, sales, cashiers |
| Day 4 | Cashiers, accounting, night audit |
| Day 5 | Housekeeping, all staff |

Staff attend the sessions relevant to their role plus Day 1 (universal foundation) and Day 5 wrap-up.

## Trainer expectations

- Trainer arrives on property Day 0 (afternoon) for setup
- Day 1 morning: introduction and foundation
- Day 5 afternoon: open Q&A and handover
- Live support typically continues 1 to 2 days post-training depending on contract

## Property responsibilities

- Confirm digital learning completion via TAP at least 7 days before trainer arrival
- Schedule staff coverage so trainees can attend without operational disruption
- Provide training room with projector, whiteboard, and workstations for each trainee
- Ensure trainees have OPERA Cloud login credentials and assigned roles before Day 1
- Identify property champion who shadows the trainer and absorbs additional content

## Post-training handover

By end of Day 5:
- Property champion confirmed and contactable
- All trainees signed off via TAP
- Outstanding questions logged for live support follow-up
- Confirmation of training completion documented for project closure

## Tiago notes

- The 40-hour schedule assumes a mid-sized property with full PMS, blocks, cashiering, and housekeeping. Limited-service or PMS-only sites may compress to 24 to 32 hours.
- OSEM properties require an additional 16 to 24 hours on top of this schedule. Plan for 7-day delivery if OSEM is in scope.
- Day 4 (Cashiering and Night Audit) is the most operationally critical. Schedule the strongest cashiers and the night auditor for this day specifically.
- The Q&A block on Day 5 should not be filled with new content. Resist the urge to use it for catch-up. Property staff need the open time to ask the awkward questions they did not ask in front of colleagues earlier in the week.
- For multi-language properties, request a translator or bilingual trainer in advance. Oracle typically delivers in English; not all EMEA properties have full English fluency on the floor.
- For partner-managed rollouts, training is often split across two trainers (operational and configuration). Coordinate calendars early.
